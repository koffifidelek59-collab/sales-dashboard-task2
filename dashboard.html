Conclusions

Key insights
  1. Revenue is concentrated in products but not in customers: the top ten items carry 33% of sales, while the top fifth of accounts carry only 25%, against 20% under perfect equality.
  2. 34.4% of order lines carry no customer key, so every customer figure on this page describes two thirds of the business.
  3. Seasonality is real but moderate: the strongest calendar month outsells the weakest by a factor of 1.33 on complete years.
  4. Price and quantity move against each other only weakly (r = -0.14), so revenue per line is driven by price far more than by volume.
Recommendations
  1. Trace the unattributed order lines back to their source system. Until that gap is closed, no segmentation or lifetime value measure can be trusted.
  2. Do not build a key-account programme on this base. The concentration curve says there are no key accounts; concentrate effort on the ten leading products instead.
  3. Do not use volume discounting as a revenue lever. The correlation structure says it will not recover the revenue it gives up.
  4. Flag negative-margin lines automatically at invoicing. At 0.41% of profit this is a control issue, not a reason to reopen pricing.
